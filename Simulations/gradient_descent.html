How it works

The update rule

At each step, compute the gradient ∇L at the current position and move opposite to it by a step size η (learning rate).

Learning rate matters

Too small → slow convergence. Too large → the ball overshoots the valley and may diverge. Try cranking it up!

Non-convex surfaces

In Multiple minima mode, where you start determines where you land. Neural networks live on surfaces like this.
