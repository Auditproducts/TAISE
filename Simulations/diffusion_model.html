How it works

Forward process q

Gradually add Gaussian noise over T steps. Each step mixes the signal with β noise until particles become pure noise at t=T.

Reverse process p

A neural network learns to predict and remove the noise added at each step — effectively running time backwards from chaos to structure.

Noise schedule

linear adds noise evenly. cosine preserves signal longer early on — empirically better for images. quadratic adds noise aggressively at start.
